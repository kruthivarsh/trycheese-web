# Surface brief — `/` (marketing home)

Mode: **Persuade**. Audience: first-time visitors from social/store-adjacent links, phone and desktop. Job: believe the photos look like real film and that trycheese is coming to iPhone **and** Android; action = notice the coming-soon store badges. Proof on hand: none real yet (placeholders labelled). Constraints: no backend, no invented ratings/reviews/numbers, Lampa's structure pinned by the user but must not read as a copy; not gloomy.

Direction source: **user-pinned** (lampa.camera structure + trycheese brand package). The concept-seed roll did not run — the impeccable launcher is quarantined by macOS — and a pinned direction beats the roll in any case.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a darkroom session: photos arrive undeveloped and come up as you look at them. It refuses the category default of a static hero photo with feature tiles; here the reveal is the experience.

OWN-WORLD: Darkroom warm black with Paper-print sections and one Safelight-red room; Bricolage Grotesque wordmark and display, Instrument Serif italic for the emotional half of each headline, Instrument Sans body, IBM Plex Mono film-edge rebate markings on every frame; rounded print frames, flat look cards with one colour bar, no gradients-as-decoration.

STORY: Say cheese → it's already film. Visitor sees the film look, learns why there's no live preview and no importing (stay in the moment; a camera, not an editor), sees the looks and features, learns it is free, private and on both platforms, leaves knowing the badges are coming. (Updated 2026-10-09 after the user corrected the concept: no rolls, no multi-hour wait.)

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left wordmark nav + "Coming soon · iOS & Android" pill. One rounded full-bleed photo frame (~78vh) with rebate markings on top and bottom edges; headline bottom-left "Say cheese. / *It's already film.*" at ~8vw; store badges beneath. Directly under the frame a strip of 12 sample frames with frame numbers; picking a frame swaps the hero.

FORM: Lampa-structured film-camera landing page (user-pinned, position 1 of 1); seed key: none (launcher unavailable; pinned direction).

Signature interaction: **develop-on-view** — every photo frame enters as a dim, safelight-tinted latent image and develops to full exposure as it scrolls into view; hero frames re-develop on selection. Motion grammar: slow exposure fades (600–900 ms, ease-out), no bounces, reduced-motion shows developed frames instantly.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
